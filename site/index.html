The bids are public. Finding them is the hard part.

Counties, cities, school boards and authorities each post solicitations on their own website or on a procurement portal. If you want to bid, you have to know where to look, and look again tomorrow.

We read those pages every morning and put every open bid in one list, sorted by due date.

How it works

  1. Tell us what you do

    Pick the kinds of work you do, like roofing, catering or IT, or type your own keywords. Skip it and you get everything.

  2. We read the pages

    Every morning we check agency websites and email alerts, remove duplicates and sort the open bids by due date.

  3. Open your inbox

    A daily or weekly email puts your matching bids first and attaches the full list as an Excel file. Unsubscribe with one click.

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Browsing, the Excel download and the daily or weekly email are all free, and the emails have no ads.

Which agencies do you cover?

We cover a growing list of Florida counties, cities, schools and authorities. The bottom of the bid list shows every page we read and whether it worked today.

How current is the list?

It refreshes every morning, about 5:20 AM Eastern. Deadlines can change after a bid is posted, so confirm on the agency's page.

A bid is missing or wrong. What now?

Every bid links to the page it came from, and that page always has the final word. To report a mistake, reply to any of our emails.

Florida Bids

Privacy policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Florida Bids (floridabids.org) lists open bids and solicitations from Florida government agencies and sends them by email to people who sign up. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

You can browse the site without giving us anything. If you subscribe to the bid emails, we keep:

  • Your email address.
  • How often you want the email (daily or weekly).
  • The business types and keywords you choose, if any, so we can put matching bids at the top of your email.
  • When you signed up, confirmed and unsubscribed, plus a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, used only to stop automated spam sign-ups.

This list is stored on our hosting provider, Cloudflare. The emails are sent through Brevo, our email delivery service, which receives your address to deliver each message. We don't use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

How we use it

We use your information only to send you the bid emails you asked for and to confirm your address. We never sell, rent or share it with anyone else, and we never send ads.

Unsubscribing

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click stops all emails. You can also change how often you hear from us, or your business types and keywords, from the settings link in any email.

Deleting your data

To have your email address and settings removed completely, email floridabidsalerts@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" from the address you subscribed with, or reply to any Florida Bids email asking us to delete it. We'll delete your record within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Facebook

Florida Bids has a Facebook app that does one thing: it posts daily bid updates to the Florida Bids Facebook Page. It does not offer Facebook Login, and it does not collect, store or use any information about Facebook users. If you have questions about data on Facebook, contact us at the address below.

Children

Florida Bids is a business service and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions or requests: floridabidsalerts@gmail.com.
